# Privacy policy

Last change: 21 November 2026.

Tarangini is a music player for the audio files on your phone. This page says what the app does with your data. It covers the app as built from this repository and the build on the Play Store.

## What the app reads

- **Your audio files.** The app asks for permission to read the audio files on the phone. It reads their names, their tags (title, artist, album) and their cover pictures to show your library and to play the files. This data stays on the phone.
- **What you do in the app.** Favourites, playlists, the play history, the queue and your settings are stored on the phone, so the app can show them again. You can clear the play history in Settings. Removing the app removes all of it.

## What leaves the phone

- **The app built from this repository** sends nothing to us or to anyone else. It has no account, no ads and no usage statistics.
- **The Play Store build** also sends a crash report when the app fails: the place in the code where it failed, the model of the phone, the Android version, the state of the phone at that moment (free memory and storage, screen direction) and a random id of the installation. The id is not tied to your name or account. A report holds no file names, no titles and nothing of your library. We use the reports only to fix faults.
- **Android Auto and voice assistants** get the titles of your songs and playlists when you browse or ask for music through them, because they show the lists. They are run by Google under its own policy.

## What we never do

We do not sell or share your data, we show no ads, and we do not follow what you listen to.

## Children

The app is not made for children under 13.

## Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the change is in the history of this file. Questions go to the issue tracker of the project: https://github.com/zeevy/tarangini/issues
