# ADR 0033: Home

- Status: accepted, 21 Nov 2026.
- Rules: A9, U4.

## Context

Home is the first screen. It is a way back to what was heard last. Radio is out of the first release (decision D48), so the sections for stations are not built now.

## Decisions

| Decision | Options looked at | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Home has three sections: "Continue listening", "Recently played" (10 songs) and "Playlists" (10). A section with nothing in it is not shown | Favourite songs as a fourth section. Rows that scroll sideways | Favourites have their own tab one tap away. A plain list is easy to read and to reach with one thumb |
| "Continue listening" is the item that is loaded in the player while it does not play, with one button to go on. It is not shown while music plays | Show it always, with play and pause | While music plays the mini player shows the same item right below. After a restart the saved queue is loaded and paused, so the card is there |
| With nothing heard, no playlist and nothing loaded, Home shows a welcome text and a button that opens the Library tab | Empty sections with a hint each | A new install has one thing to do: find the songs. The audio permission is asked in the Library tab only |
| Home reads `PlayHistory`, `PlaylistRepository` and the player state. It reads again after a counted play, a changed playlist and a rescan | A use case that joins the lists | Two short reads by an index, with no join of their results |
| The frame of Home (`HomeScreen`) takes what it shows as a slot, and the app puts `HomeSections` into it | `HomeScreen` builds its own ViewModel | The tests of the app shell run without Hilt, and Home is the first screen they open. The mini player is handed in the same way |
| A tap on a recent song plays the recent songs from that song on. A tap on a playlist goes to the Library tab and opens the playlist on top of it, so Back leads to the lists | Play the song alone | The same rule as every other list of songs |

## What follows

- The app has one dark look (decision D51), so the screenshot tests have one picture per state.
- Home shows no spinner: the two reads take a moment, and a spinner would only flash.
- When the saved queue is the only thing the app has (no history, no playlist), the welcome text can show for a moment before the player is reached and the card comes.
- Recent stations and favourite stations come back with radio.
