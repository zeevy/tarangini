# Contributor agreement (draft)

> [!WARNING]
> This text is a draft. A lawyer has not reviewed it yet. It may change before the repo accepts outside contributions.

This agreement is between you and Silicon Circuits ("the project owner"), the owner of the Tarangini project. It covers every contribution you send to the project: code, docs, images and any other work, sent as a pull request or in any other way.

## 1. You keep your copyright

You keep the copyright and every other right in your contribution. This agreement only gives the project owner the rights listed below.

## 2. Copyright licence

You give the project owner a licence to your contribution that is perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, free of charge and cannot be taken back. Under it the project owner may copy, change, publish, perform and distribute your contribution and works made from it, and may license it to others under any terms. This includes open source licences other than the Mozilla Public License 2.0, and closed licences.

## 3. Patent licence

If you hold a patent that your contribution, alone or together with the project, would infringe, you give the project owner and everyone who receives the project a licence to that patent that is perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, free of charge and cannot be taken back. It covers making, using, selling and distributing the contribution as part of the project.

## 4. What you confirm

- The contribution is your own work, or you have the right to send it under this agreement.
- If your employer has rights in what you write, your employer has allowed you to send it, or has given up those rights for this contribution.
- You have told us about any third party code, licence, patent or other limit that you know applies to your contribution.
- You send no code under GPL or AGPL, and nothing you have no right to share.

## 5. No warranty

You give your contribution "as is", with no warranty of any kind, unless the law requires one.

## 6. How the project uses your contribution

The project owner publishes the public code of Tarangini under the Mozilla Public License 2.0. The project owner is not required to use your contribution.

## How to agree

Tick the agreement line in the pull request form. By doing so you agree to this text for that pull request and for every later contribution you send.
